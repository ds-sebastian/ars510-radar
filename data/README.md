# Data

## `sample/`: real CAN captures

Three short highway captures, used by the tests and figures:

| file | length | content |
|---|---|---|
| `highway_following_30s.csv.gz` | 30 s | drive A: following a lead at ~75 m (55-86 m) while a left-lane car closes from ~88 m to ~45 m |
| `highway_vrel_excursion_25s.csv.gz` | 25 s | drive A: a settled lead at ~48 m, slowly opening (41 → 57 m over the capture), whose over-ground speed falls 33.5 → 25.0 m/s within ~1.4 s and recovers within ~1.2 s ([docs/07](../docs/07_velocity_excursions.md)) |
| `highway_acc_anchor_24s.csv.gz` | 24 s | drive E: includes the radar's ACC target (0x235 / 0x237); from ~17 s the lead's object-list speed falls to about −6 m/s and its range slides 46 → 32 m in 2 s while the ACC target holds −0.75 to −0.9 m/s until ~18.7 s ([docs/12](../docs/12_kalman_filter.md#the-model)) |

Format `t_s,bus,address,data_hex`, times rebased to 0. The rows are bus 1 0x80 / 0x81 / 0x85 / 0x86 / 0x192 (and 0x235 / 0x237 in
the drive E sample) and bus 0 0xB4 frames: CAN bytes at relative times.

## `analysis/`: anonymised analysis dataset

Three drives: **A** (development, 24 min decoded, mixed), **B** (city, 40 min), **C** (highway, 24 min). Segment labels
`A00` … `C23` are one-minute files in order; `t*` columns are seconds from each segment's first record, so the files carry anonymous
labels and relative times.

| file | rows | content |
|---|---|---|
| `slots.parquet` | 174k | every occupied 0x80 slot with age ≥ 1: decoded `x` (dRel), `y` (yRel), `vrel`, `v_ground`, `v_ego` (0xB4), `track`, `slot`, `age`, `init_template`, and every raw field (`DREL`, `YREL_LEFT`, `VLONG_OVER_GROUND`, …, `UNK_<start>_<len>`) in the original survey's windows ([`slot_bit_map.json`](reference/slot_bit_map.json)); [docs/03](../docs/03_slot_fields.md) gives the current field widths, e.g. `256\|8` and `264\|8` |
| `camera_pairs.parquet` | 90k | radar tracks paired by bearing with narrow-camera YOLO boxes (radar `y` here and in `ground_contact` is lateral code / 64; × 0.96 gives metres, the 0.015 m per code unit): box (`x1..y2`, `h`), box-growth closing speed over 2 / 4 / 6 s (`vcam2/4/6`), 1 s range derivative (`deriv1s`), 10 s range slope, ego speed from 0xB4 / carState / GPS, lead flag |
| `ground_contact.parquet` | 50k | camera ground-contact distance (`cam_ground_x`, flat-road projection) against radar `x` |
| `lateral_pairs.parquet` | 22k | camera lateral estimates against the raw lateral code |
| `lane_cells.parquet` | 48k | camera-matched 0x85 lane-curve cell rows (cells 2/3/8/9): offset, heading, curvature codes and bit 79, camera lane slope and curvature, ego-motion offset rate; segment labels are an index within this sample |
| `standstill_codes.parquet` | 15k | `64\|10` codes while ego is stopped; includes moving targets, with a dominant low-speed peak |
| `brake_events/E*_*.csv` | | drive-A brake-event windows: replay ticks, radar lead track, camera pair |
| `fault_injection.csv` | 82 | ghost / fault scenarios through radard and the planner |
| `summary_owner_case.csv` | 476 | relative-time owner-drive O1 comparison under `fused` 2.0: summaries off / on, and captured vision; saved planner and lead outputs, anonymous lead T1 |
| `acc_function_tracker.parquet` | 60.6k | second car: one 37 s curve (ACC target, matching summary, nearest object-list track, camera lead, 0x239 object-list flag; relative time) and 20,000-row samples of ACC range steps vs speed × cycle time and of summary / object-list range minus ACC; figure `acc_function_tracker` |
| `lead_choice_cases.csv.gz` | 500 | two road moments for [docs/00](../docs/00_start_here.md#6-choosing-which-tracks-openpilot-sees) (`case`, relative `t`): planner request and published lead (range, radar flag) under vision only, `fused` 2.1 and 2.3 (2.4 asks the same), plus the camera lead range, the radar's ACC target range and ego speed; owner drives, anonymised |
| `revealed_queue_case.csv.gz` | 1327 | one road moment for [docs/17](../docs/17_upstream.md#against-vision-only-what-the-pr-adds-over-today) (`series`, `t` relative to the ACC target's switch to a revealed slow car): logged camera-lead and ACC-target speed and distance, ego speed; closed-loop replay request, ego speed and gap under `acc`, `acc_summaries` and vision only; owner drive, anonymised |
| `stats.json`, `STATS.md` | | descriptive statistics from `tools/compute_stats.py` |

`slots.parquet` is radar-only. The camera files are references with their own limits: tracker swaps at range, hood
clipping below ~8 m, a flat-road assumption, and metric camera velocity that takes its scale from radar range.

`init_template` marks **869 allocation placeholders** among the 173,691 rows: age 1 with zero length and width
codes. Their `x = 0` is a placeholder: position, velocity and physical-object statistics use the rows with `init_template` false,
raw allocation/lifecycle analysis uses all rows. The decoder withholds these rows from publication.
`tools/mark_init_templates.py` adds or refreshes this column after a dataset rebuild; the dataset counts are in
[`init_templates_dataset.json`](analysis/summaries/init_templates_dataset.json).

## `reference/units.json`

The current unit, zero code and confidence of every scaled field (object-list slot fields, uncertainty codes, ACC target
signals), the 0xB4 ego factor per car and the radar cycle (60.28 ms), plus retired values with the reason they were
retired. A correction starts here: `tests/test_units_ledger.py` fails until `ars510/`, the profile files and docs/03
agree with it, and fails when a retired value reappears in the code.

## `reference/slot_bit_map.json`

Per-bit statistics of the 0x80 slot (288 bits), the 0x80 header and the 0x85 record from 13 one-minute segments of
drive A: flip rates, one-rates and the automatic carry-chain field split. `tools/build_cabana_route.py` builds the
Cabana DBC's slot layout from it.

[`register_structure_and_reference_scope.json`](analysis/summaries/register_structure_and_reference_scope.json) supplies the three-car slow-register layout/update counts, near-anchor range comparisons and original-transmission 0x680 copy residuals used in docs/05 and docs/06. Unit-identifying payloads and hashes stay private.

[`car_bus_0x366_distance.json`](analysis/summaries/car_bus_0x366_distance.json) supplies the 0x366 distance and lateral rules in docs/13: floor(range / 0.79 m) and trunc(−lateral / 0.32 m) against the ACC target on three car groups, with the units' sharpness and the inexact speed fit.

[`car_bus_0x33e_boundary.json`](analysis/summaries/car_bus_0x33e_boundary.json) supplies the code-7 idle/numeric-domain witness in docs/13: finite TTC and a live matching target at high reported positive acceleration. Saturation remains a candidate interpretation.

[`slot_length_eight_bits.json`](analysis/summaries/slot_length_eight_bits.json) supplies the length-width evidence in docs/03: the length-code tail through 127 → 128 and the classes of codes ≥ 128 on the owner car and three other car groups.

[`id85_countdown_units.json`](analysis/summaries/id85_countdown_units.json) supplies the 0x85 prefix count-down rates in docs/04: bytes 9 / 11 per metre driven and bytes 13 / 14 per second, by ego-speed band and log group.

[`slot_bit182_lateral_pedestrian.json`](analysis/summaries/slot_bit182_lateral_pedestrian.json) supplies the slot bit 182 numbers in docs/03: pedestrian share and the lateral-speed / side contrast with the car stopped, owner and second car, plus the fleet pedestrian share.

[`slot_bit106_outside_fov.json`](analysis/summaries/slot_bit106_outside_fov.json) supplies the slot bit 106 numbers in docs/03: the measured-row azimuth edge by range and the bit-106 share among predicted rows by azimuth and range, three car groups.

[`range_sigma_odometry.json`](analysis/summaries/range_sigma_odometry.json) supplies the `224|7` odometry numbers in docs/03: stationary-object range residual by code band on two cars and on about 200 public fleet cars, with the σ-vs-variance comparison, plus the fleet `232|8` lateral and `240|7` speed scatter of the same stationary objects.

[`learned_alignment_501_502.json`](analysis/summaries/learned_alignment_501_502.json) supplies the 0x501 / 0x502 co-variation in docs/05: within-car Spearman and slope of 0x502 byte 1 against the camera's 0x501 code over the public fleet.
